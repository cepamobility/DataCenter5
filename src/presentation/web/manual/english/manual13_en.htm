BASIC INSTRUCTIONS ON ACCESS AND USE
ACTIVITIES
 
The Activities item on the Menu branches off as follows.

 
Activities/Management
 
Allows users to add, supervise, edit or delete training sessions. The options “Supervise” and “Delete” are exclusive for CEPA.
 
Activities/Reports
 
Shows activity reports sorted by result and driver.
 
Activities/Queries
 
Allows users to query expired, planned and completed activities by type of training, driver, period, and division.
